XRR Fitter Qt Cocoa source derivative

Based on Qt 6.11.2 Cocoa platform sources published by The Qt Company Ltd.
The source files retain their original SPDX identifiers and copyright notices.
The upstream PySide6/Qt license notices remain unchanged in this wheel.

XRR changes: native accessibility snapshot ownership and invalidation, supplied
as ownership.patch alongside the original Cocoa source files and build recipe.
This is a locally built arm64 derivative, not an unmodified PyPI release.
The source manifest records the official source/SDK URLs and SHA-256 digests.
The Apple toolchain and unchanged Qt frameworks are external build inputs.

To rebuild, use tools/qt_cocoa_build.py from the matching XRR Fitter source
checkout. Do not apply the patch by modifying an installed wheel in place.
The private Qt ABI requires Qt 6.11.2. Do not relabel this output universal2.
